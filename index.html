Word of the Day

reticent

adjective

Someone may be described as reticent if they are feeling or showing reluctance, that is, hesitation or unwillingness to do something. Reticent can also describe someone who is reserved, inclined to be silent or uncommunicative about things.

Use of reticent to mean “reluctant” is well established. The word comes from the Latin reticēre, meaning “to keep silent.”
